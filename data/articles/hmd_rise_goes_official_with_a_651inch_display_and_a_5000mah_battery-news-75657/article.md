# HMD Rise goes official with a 6.51-inch display and a 5,000mAh battery

Barely a day after its full specifications leaked online, the HMD Rise has officially launched in India. The phone comes with features including a 5,000mAh battery and a single 13MP rear camera.

The affordable 4G handset is powered by a Unisoc 9863A octa-core chipset, which is paired with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage. Luckily, the storage is expandable up to 128GB via a microSD card.

It has a 6.517-inch LCD display up front with a waterdrop notch that houses a 5MP selfie camera. At the back, the phone features a 13MP primary camera and an LED flash.

The HMD Rise has a 5,000mAh battery, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and runs Android 16 Go Edition. HMD is promising 2 years of security updates and you get the Indus AI assistant that supports 22 Indian languages.

Pricing wise, the Rise will be available via Flipkart from December 2 onwards for INR 9,999.

It is being offered in Emerald Gold, Indigo Silver, and Midnight color options. The HMD Rise features a dual-tone design.
