# Meta Muse Charm is a standalone AI device coming later this year

Right after unveiling the new camera-less Ray-Ban Meta Audio and Meta VR glasses, Mark Zuckerberg went to a “one-more thing” device – the Meta Muse Charm. As the name implies, this is a standalone keychain-like/Tamagotchi type device that allows users to interact with Meta’s Muse AI.

Meta Muse Charm

From the sound of it, the device is in its late development phase, but Zuckerberg did confirm that it should ship in time for the holidays in December. Muse Charm supports voice input and features interchangeable avatars.

It also gets a front-facing camera, a fingerprint sensor on the top right corner and an action button on the left side. Meta said it “packed the whole Muse experience” inside the Muse Charm with real-time voice, so it should be able to do everything you can on the standard Muse AI app.
