# Amazon US Black Friday deals: the best smartphone offers

Amazon extended Black Friday sales to a full week – but those seven days are nearly over. The big date, the actual Black Friday, is tomorrow, so you don’t have much time left. We looked through the offers and updated the prices in a couple of places. We’re glad to report that nothing is out of stock (well, aside from a Galaxy Xcover7 Pro).

We should note that some of the Samsung deals have clocks on them and there are less than 24 hours remaining for them, so they may time out before Black Friday is over.

The Samsung Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra are not technically part of the Black Friday sale, but the Galaxy S26 Ultra discount is bigger than it was last week, so we’re including them. Also note the S26 and S26+ prices – they are important for context when the Pixel 11 models and the S26 FE get involved.

Speaking of the Pixels, we got used to middling discounts – not this week, though. The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is 31% off and you can get an extra $325 if you trade in an old phone. The small Google Pixel 11 Pro has a similar discount (-32%) and $300 trade-in bonus. We’ll see the vanilla Pixel 11, but we’ll tell you right now that it’s only $50 less than the Pro and it’s not worth the downgrade.

Since Amazon doesn’t sell the iPhone Duo or the Moto Razr Fold, this category is left with only the two Samsungs – the Galaxy Z Fold8 and the Z Fold8 Ultra. The discounts aren’t bad, but the phones cost $50 more than they did last week.

The flip category has more variety as Motorola joins the party. The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip8 sets the tone with a base price of $1,000, which puts it just $50 below the S26 Ultra. Of course, it’s all about the form factor with the Z Flip.

The Motorola Razr Ultra 2026 is $300 more, which is a tough sell when the very similar Razr Ultra 2025 is $600 cheaper. Also, the vanilla Razr 2026 may not fare well compared to the older Razr+ models below, not to mention that it costs the same as last year’s Ultra.

The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE has fallen to its best price yet – we would recommend getting the 256GB variant as 128GB will be tight in the long run. Remember that this phone is slated for 7 years of OS updates. Also, it has a telephoto camera, which is rare in this price range. Scroll back up and compare it with the Galaxy S26+.

The Google Pixel 11, as we mentioned above, is only $50 less than the equivalent Pixel 11 Pro. If you want a 6.3” Pixel, the Pro is the one to get.

The Nothing Phone (3) and (4a) Pro are a breath of fresh air in the US market, which is dominated by four brands. The (3) is a 2025 model, but it has better hardware than its similarly priced 2026 mid-range sibling. Both have 50MP telephoto cameras, though, compared to the S26 FE’s 8MP.

The Samsung Galaxy A37 is around $100 cheaper than the S26 FE, but you will be missing out on a lot of performance (Exynos 1480 vs. 2500), the telephoto camera and the 15W wireless charging.

If you were looking for a phone that is easily repairable or one that is good for reading, the two below are worth your consideration.
