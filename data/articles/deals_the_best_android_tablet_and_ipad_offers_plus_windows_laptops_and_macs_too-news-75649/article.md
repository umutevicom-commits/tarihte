# Deals: the best Android tablet and iPad offers, plus Windows laptops and Macs too

We’ve already looked at the top smartphone deals from Amazon Germany that are on offer for Black Friday week. Now, let’s do the same for tablets, laptops and even some computers. Samsung has both Android tablets and Windows laptops, while Apple has iPadOS tablets and a couple of Macs. There are also Amazon’s latest Android tablets and Microsoft’s Windows-on-ARM devices.

The Samsung tablet deals mostly feature older models – like the two previous Tab S Ultra versions and the small Tab S11. That one is still current, though, as the Tab S12 series only has an Ultra and a Plus.

Samsung’s Windows laptops run on a selection of Intel Core Ultra Series 3 CPUs (Panther Lake). The lower-end models have IPS displays (with or without touch), while the more premium Pro model switches to 120Hz OLED.

The iPad Air models offer a good mix of features and performance. However, they still have 60Hz IPS LCDs. If you want the new 120Hz Tandem OLED displays, you’ll have to spend iPad Pro money. There are cheaper options like the vanilla iPad and the old iPad mini.

This Apple Mac Mini uses the latest M6 chipset. It has a 2.5Gb Ethernet port, 3x Thunderbolt ports and an HDMI port on the back, plus 2x USB-C ports on the front. The Apple iMac also has the M6, but getting more than two ports costs extra – consider buying a USB hub instead. Also, that’s a lot of money for an all-in-one computer, but note that you get a high-quality 24” 4.5K IPS LCD, a Magic Keyboard and a Magic Mouse. The keyboard and mouse alone will cost you €150 if bought separately.

Amazon recently launched a series of Alexa tablets – these run proper Android (Google Play Store and all) and have premium builds featuring an aluminum chassis. Note that the Alexa Tablet 12 Pro comes without ads by default (but that is not the case for the other two). Also, we’ve linked the version with a glare-resistant Nanomatte display – it’s only a €10 upgrade and we think it’s well worth it. This version is also quite powerful by Amazon tablet standards with a Dimensity 8400 chipset.

The Amazon Kids Tablet comes in two sizes and a kid-proof protective case. This one doesn’t have ads and includes a free year of Kids+, which offers a curated selection of books, videos, games and apps for kids. It also has access to the Google Play Store for when kiddo grows up.

The Microsoft Surface Pro is a Windows-on-ARM tablet – this version comes without the optional keyboard. If a keyboard is what you’re after, you may as well pick up the Surface Laptop instead.
