# Apple reportedly revamping notifications once again with iOS 28

Apple has revamped its iOS notification system several times over the years, including the infamous AI-generated notification summaries that were introduced with iOS 18. Now, the company is reportedly preparing another overhaul with iOS 28.

While the exact nature of the iOS 28 notifications overhaul remains unknown, MacRumors’ Joe Rossignol claims to have heard from multiple sources that Apple is planning a revamp for next year.

The sources reportedly said that the Notification Center in iOS 28 will receive significant changes, but stopped short of sharing any further details. As with any early rumor, it's best to take this with a grain of salt.

Apple is currently testing iOS 27.3, which is in beta. The company is expected to unveil iOS 28 at WWDC next year, where it will also showcase the new features coming to the next version of its mobile operating system.
