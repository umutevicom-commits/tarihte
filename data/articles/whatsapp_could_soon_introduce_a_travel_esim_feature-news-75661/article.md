# WhatsApp could soon introduce a travel eSIM feature

WhatsApp is reportedly working on a new travel eSIM feature that would let users discover, purchase, install, and manage eSIMs for their travel destinations directly through the app.

The travel eSIM feature was spotted in the latest WhatsApp for Android version 2.26.47:10. According to Android Authority, the app contains several references to the feature, although it isn't live yet.

Some of the text strings associated with the feature include "Travel eSIM installed", "Your plan starts when you turn on your eSIM", and "Plans aren't available for this destination".

Based on these strings, users will likely be able to search for a destination within WhatsApp and browse supported eSIM plans. They could then purchase and install an eSIM directly through the app. However, references to a "Coming soon" message suggest that the feature may not be available in all regions.

The findings also suggest that WhatsApp could partner with a third-party eSIM provider to offer the service. Additionally, the feature will require a phone with eSIM support. Devices without an eSIM chip or the necessary Android software support may not be able to access or use it.

Having a built-in way to discover and install eSIMs would make WhatsApp even more useful, particularly for travellers. We hope the feature rolls out officially soon.
