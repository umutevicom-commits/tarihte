# GSMArena 2026 Yıl Sonu Ödülleri finalistlerinin duyurulması

2026 bize bazı inanılmaz akıllı telefonlar, tabletler ve diğer cihazlar getirdi ve bunların en iyisini tanımak istedik – işte GSMArena 2026 Yıl Sonu Ödülleri Adayları. On üç kategori var ve kazananlar önümüzdeki günlerde belirlenecek.

Uzman inceleme ekibimiz son on iki ay içinde 200 'den fazla cihazı test etti. Ağırlıklı olarak telefonlara odaklandık, ancak bu yılın en iyi tabletini, akıllı saatini ve akıllı aksesuarını da ödüllendireceğiz.

Hangi cihazların aday gösterileceğine karar verdik ve şu anda her kategori için kazananlar üzerinde tartışıyoruz. Bu arada, hangi modellerin kazanmayı hak ettiğine dair görüşlerinizi duymak istedik.

İlk olarak, kategorilere hızlı bir giriş. Büyük ödül Yılın Telefonu, ancak farklı fiyat aralıkları ve farklı nişler için kategoriler var – örneğin, en iyi pil ömrüne sahip telefon veya en iyi oyun cihazı. Ayrıca 2026 'da tasarım mükemmelliği ve yılın en iyi donanım yeniliği için adaylarımız var.

Daha fazla uzatmadan, her kategoride GSMArena 2026 Yıl Sonu Ödülü finalistlerini sunuyoruz. Belirli bir sırada listelenmediklerini unutmayın.

Bu dört telefon, performans, dayanıklılık, ekran kalitesi ve kamera becerisi arasında en iyi dengeyi sunar.

Ayrıca, efsanevi kamera üreticisinin kamerayı ve özelliklerini ayarlamada eli olduğu için Hasselblad logosunu gururla taşıyor.

VIVO X500 Pro Max, ZEISS T* kaplamalı lenslere sahip olağanüstü bir kameraya sahiptir. Tüm testlerimizde harika performans gösterdi, ancak bizi uçuran video sabitleme oldu - eşiti yok. Dimensity 9600 Pro çip ve büyük bir Si/C pil, mükemmel performans ve dayanıklılık sunar ve ekran mükemmeldir.

You may not know this, but many on our team got into photography long before phones could snap a photo, so this category is near and dear to our hearts. Anyone with a passion for photography and videography – and the budget to buy the necessary hardware – will be best-served by one of these.

With two 200MP cameras and two 50MP cameras on its back, the Oppo Find X9 Ultra leaves nothing to chance - it will have the right focal length to capture every special moment, even if it happens far away (that 10x/230mm lens has a long reach).

You don't need a 1"-type sensor to compete in this category, but it sure helps. That's not all, the Xiaomi 17 Ultra also equips its 200MP telephoto camera with a variable focal length lens (75-100mm, 3.2-4.3x) for continuous zoom.

While the Oppo and Xiaomi excel at photography, their video recording skills left something to be desired. The vivo X300 Ultra is loaded with video features, including Dolby Vision recording and superb stabilization.

While it is true that the smartphone era has been defined by the “black rectangle” design, there are a few brands that went above and beyond to make their phones stand out. Here are the four phones that have a unique look.

The Nothing Phone (4a) Pro is instantly recognizable to everyone familiar with the brand - everyone else is sure to ask you what phone you are using. In a time when smartphone makers rush to copy every single design cue from one another, having a unique look is rare.

Car buffs know the name Pininfarina well and every time the design studio puts its spin on a smartphone, the results grab attention. That is the case for the Infinix Note 60 Ultra, even if you don't go for the Lamborghini edition.

What's your favorite color? When it comes to the Oppo Find X9 Ultra, ours is Tundra Umber. It's even a different design to the other X9 Ultra color options, with the subtle changes going a long way towards making this feel like a crossbreed between a phone and a Hasselblad camera.

The gaming aesthetic of the RedMagic 12 Pro gives it a futuristic look and transparent panels offer enticing glimpses of the unique components inside, including a micro-pump that moves real cooling liquid.

It feels like smartphones pull the rest of the electronics industry forward – they drive innovations in semiconductor, display, camera and battery tech along with other fields like sensors. These are the phones that brought something new, something that we might (and probably will) see in future smartphones and, perhaps, in other electronics too.

The Honor Robot Phone looks like CGI in videos - did they really build a retractable gimbal for the camera? They really did! We're not sure whether all the AI features are just buzzwords, but the gimbal allows this phone's camera to do things no other phone can.

Your phone may be the most secure device you own. This is why curious eyes might be tempted to peep at the verification code you just received - the Privacy Display on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra will keep your secrets, though.

Foldables looked impossibly futuristic, now they are quite common. What's next - tri-foldables maybe? Well, maybe, maybe not. But if tri-foldables are the future, this is where they started - with the Huawei Mate XT 2 Ultimate.

The bezels around smartphone screens are a modern retelling of Zeno's paradox - they get slimmer but never disappear completely. But what if they can? The Tecno bezelless concept explores ways to do just that.

Printing a document on paper is outdated and wasteful, but it is kinder on your eyes than typical digital displays. The TCL P80 Ultra adapts the company's long-running NxtPaper technology to AMOLED displays to bring the best of both worlds.

Tablets have carved several niches – media consumption, work and learning – that aren’t covered by either your phone or your laptop. These are the three best all-round tablets of the year.

It's big, it's powerful, what more can you want? The Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra benefits from the highly developed DeX software that offers desktop-like multitasking, which pairs perfectly with the optional keyboard and maybe even external displays. The Tab S12 Ultra can handle anything you throw at it.

The Honor MagicPad4 strikes a good balance between price and performance - it costs less than half as much as the Galaxy and while it can't match it on screen size or performance, it gets close enough for most use-cases. It can handle up to 20 windows running simultaneously!

The OnePlus Pad 4 is what we loved about OnePlus in tablet form - it's sad to see the company leave the global market since now most people around the world will be missing out on devices such as this. It's sleek, powerful, well-priced and we liked the new keyboard and stylus that the company designed.

Smartwatches are indispensable for anyone who wants around-the-clock health tracking, who exercises regularly and/or who must have instant access to their notifications even if their phone is in the other room. So, in a way, your choice of smartwatch is more personal than your choice of smartphone – these are our top five picks, but only one will be chosen as the best.

The nano-crystal ceramic bezel was a great addition to the recognizable octagon design of the Huawei Watch GT 7 Pro as it enhances the premium look that the GT series is known for. It's a daily-wear watch that lasted us 15 days in testing while tracking all our exercise sessions.

Of the three classes of smartwatch that Samsung makes, the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra is both the most rugged and the most capable one. It has a rich set of health-tracking features and the excellent Samsung Health app and is perfectly equipped for any outdoor adventure.

Wear OS watches are packed with features, but watches that use simpler software offer longer battery life. What if you could have both? The Xiaomi Watch 5 lasted a full 5 days with all-day health tracking and notifications in our testing. It does so with one simple trick - a massive 930mAh Si/C battery (we've owned phones with smaller batteries!). The watch has a premium, understated design and a secondary OS for lower power operation if you need to go more than 5 days between charges.

While the Oppo Watch X3 doesn't have the huge battery of the Xiaomi (it has 646mAh capacity), it consistently lasted 4 days of use with AOD enabled. The fast 10W charging system gets it to around 50% in 15 minutes and to a full 100% in under an hour. This one also has a simple secondary OS for longer operation when you don't need the full Wear OS suite of features. The X3 isn't all about battery, the premium titanium body and sapphire glass, plus the cool Dynamic-Link strap elevate it above your typical smartwatch.

There is a rich ecosystem of accessories that surrounds the smartphone market. This is in part the result of manufacturers stripping the retail package to the bone – all you get these days is a USB cable, if that – and in part because some of these accessories enable unique features. Here are the ones that we think would add the most value to your smartphone experience.

Unless you have one of the phones in our Best Battery category, battery anxiety is still around. The Xiaomi UltraThin Magnetic Power Bank 5000 is the most compact way to alleviate it - it measures just 6mm thick (compared to 7.7mm for Apple's Air MagSafe battery) and weighs 98g (vs. 126g). The 15W rating only applies to Xiaomi phones, everything else gets 7.5W. Still, it brought a nearly dead iPhone 15 Pro to 91% before depleting its charge.

Chargers can be something that hides under your desk or behind your nightstand or they can be something that's full of character. Now, we're too old to be playing with Tamagotchis, but the TORRAS FlashEye Dot Smart Display Charger 45W is undeniably cute. And it's a compact, relatively fast charger that you can use for phones and tablets at home or while you travel.

Geeks tend to have a lot of devices that need charging - some of them pretty power-hungry too. The UGREEN Nexode Pro 300W Smart Display Desktop Charger has six ports and can output a combined 300W with its top USB-C port reaching 100W. This can charge a house worth of gadgets all at once.

If you have fewer gadgets or need more power for one beefy gadget, the Anker Prime 160W offers three USB-C ports, one of which can deliver 140W. While the UGREEN is very much an at-home charger, the Anker is compact enough to travel and will feed even a top-end MacBook at your hotel.

Speaking of Tamagotchis, cough, speaking of travel, the OPPO Bubble Mag E-Badge is a tiny vlog and selfie helper - it's a display that can mangetically attach to the back of your phone, acting as a viewfinder for the main cameras. It also works as a remote control from up to 10m away, giving you complete freedom to frame your shot. And, you know, the custom Vibe E-Badge designs are pretty fun.
